# Glim Demo
- You're a girl, walking home at night nobody around you. What can you do?
- Our app helps you set timers that check up on you (make fake calls to your device). If you dont pick up notification gets triggered to your safe contacts.
- Furthermore, we make use of Vapi voice to stimulate 2 conversations that are recorded and sent to database. You can export them and they can be sent to your contact with your geolocation.
- Ai voices will speak endlessly and they will never give a hint that something is wrong or that they are fake.
- You can select a safe word for Mum Ai voice, and that safe word currently is "bestie".
- When you say word bestie, currently the alarm is triggered, but voice continues speaking normally.
- It is connected to database and transcription is shown in the app and it can also be downloaded.

find it here: https://glim-demo.onrender.com/
